The company

About Bikenest Technologies

BIKENEST TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED is an Indian software company incorporated to design, operate, and support consumer marketplaces. We serve riders across India — from Kashmir to Kanyakumari.

The company builds products that remove middlemen from used two-wheeler trade. We do not buy or sell vehicles ourselves, take a broker’s cut, or act as a dealer. We provide the technology so individual owners can list, search, message, and complete a sale in person.

Our public website is bikenest.in. Sveyo is the mobile application we publish for this marketplace on iOS and Android.

Simple process

From listing to handshake in four steps

  1. 1

    Post or browse

    Sellers list with photos and details. Buyers search nearby used bikes by brand, price, and mileage.

  2. 2

    Message directly

    Chat with the owner — no broker in the middle, no hidden fees, no awkward phone calls through a third party.

  3. 3

    Meet & inspect

    Visit at a time that works for both of you. Check the bike in person before money changes hands.

  4. 4

    Close the deal

    Agree on a fair price, hand over the keys, and ride away — person to person.
